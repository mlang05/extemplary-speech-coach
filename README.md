## About Extemplary

Extemplary gives extemporaneous speakers a complete practice workflow in the browser. Generate a realistic question, work through a 30-minute preparation period, record the speech, and assess the result using transcripts, scores, and delivery-focused feedback.

Built with NSDA-style practice in mind, the workspace brings together an eight-part NSDA scoring rubric, vocal metrics, word-by-word transcription, synchronized video and transcript review, and stored ballots. This makes it possible to study both a single performance and longer-term progress across multiple sessions.

- Create realistic prompts for extemporaneous speaking drills.

## Practice Workflow

1. Open Extemplary in a compatible modern browser.
2. Sign in if you need account-based history or cloud storage.
3. Create a practice question.
4. Begin the 30-minute preparation period.
5. Allow browser audio and video access, then record the speech.
6. Submit the recording for transcription and evaluation.
7. Study the NSDA-oriented score, delivery measurements, and annotated transcript.
8. Play the recording back with transcript timing to investigate particular sections.
9. Save the ballot and compare it with previous practice attempts.
10. Export any materials you want to retain offline.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who should use Extemplary?

Extemplary is for speakers practicing NSDA extemporaneous speaking, especially those seeking a structured preparation process, recorded performance review, and rubric-based feedback.

### Is a preparation countdown included?

Yes. Every practice workflow provides a 30-minute preparation timer.

### Will it work offline?

The installable PWA can continue timing and recording without an active connection. Authentication, transcription, AI judging, and cloud storage may require internet access.

### How are practice results saved?

Signed-in use can place ballots, performance history, transcripts, and related practice information in cloud storage. The installed PWA can also rely on local browser storage for its offline functionality.

### Can the transcript be viewed during playback?

Yes. The annotated transcript is synchronized with the video, allowing you to inspect the words and corresponding moments together.

### Which delivery details are analyzed?

Vocal analysis covers characteristics including pace, pauses, filler words, and stutters.

### What can I do if recording fails?

Check that microphone and camera permissions are enabled, make sure the intended input devices are selected, and reload the application if permissions were modified after the session started.

### How do I save copies of my work?

Use the available export tools to download scores, ballots, transcripts, and recorded videos.
